Secure Your Winnings (Step-by-Step)

In the high-stakes world of cryptocurrency, security is the bedrock of trust. Learn how to move your assets to the Ledger hardware vault in under 5 minutes.

01

Initialise Your Secure Element

Connect your Ledger device to your PC or mobile. Use the official Ledger Live application to initialise the certified Secure Element chip.

"What You See Is What You Sign": Unlike software wallets, Ledger's secure screen ensures your transaction details are 100% accurate and tamper-proof.

02

Verify the Receive Address

In Ledger Live, select "Receive" and choose your asset. Your Ledger device will display the address. Always verify that every character on your monitor matches the address on your physical Ledger screen. This prevents man-in-the-middle attacks.

03

Execute the Transfer

Copy the verified address into your casino or exchange withdrawal field. We recommend sending a small "test amount" first. Once confirmed on the blockchain, move your full balance to your private vault.
