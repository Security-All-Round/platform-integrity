Privacy Protocol

01. Data Collection

SecurityAllround operates as an independent technical auditor. We do not require user registration or store personal financial records. Information submitted voluntarily via our Communications or Audit Request forms is transmitted over encrypted channels solely to respond to your inquiry.

02. Third-Party Links

Our platform contains outbound links to audited third-party gaming, custody, and security providers. Once you leave securityallround.com, you are subject to the privacy policies and terms of the destination platform.

03. Analytics & Telemetry

We use aggregate analytics (Google Analytics) and performance telemetry (Grafana Faro) to measure page load speed, Australian edge-node reachability, and outbound link reliability. You can opt out or block analytics cookies at any time via your browser settings or privacy extensions.
