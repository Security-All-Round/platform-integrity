Privacy Protocol

01. Data Collection

SecurityAllround operates as an independent technical auditor. We do not require user registration or the storage of personal financial data. The only data collected is via the "Audit Request" portal, which is transmitted via encrypted channels to our technical lead.

02. Third-Party Links

Our platform contains links to vetted third-party gaming infrastructures. Once you leave our domain, you are subject to the privacy protocols of the target platform. We recommend reviewing their specific encryption and data handling policies.

03. Cookies & Tracking

We use minimal telemetry to analyze Australian edge-node performance and site reachability. We do not use persistent tracking cookies for marketing purposes.
