Surveillance Node Audit

Evaluation of PoE infrastructure, local NVR encryption, and AI-driven perimeter detection.

01. Optical Performance & Data Integrity

Our audit focused on 4K PoE (Power over Ethernet) systems. Unlike wireless consumer cameras, wired PoE systems are immune to 2.4GHz/5GHz jammer attacks. We verified the encryption of data streams from the camera node to the local Network Video Recorder (NVR).

[+] Transmission: Wired PoE (802.3af/at)
[+] Resolution: 3840 x 2160 (4K Ultra HD)
[+] Storage: Local RAID-redundant NVR
[+] Encryption: AES-256 (Local Stream)

Tactical Verdict

We recommend local-storage PoE systems for high-fidelity perimeter protection. Cloud-dependent systems are currently failed by our data sovereignty audit.
