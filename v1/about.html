Technical Auditing & Access Research

SecurityAllround is an independent organisation dedicated to documenting the technical infrastructure and real-world accessibility of digital entertainment platforms. We focus on mitigating ISP filtering, verifying mirror resilience, and auditing secure routing paths.

Our Mission

In an era of increasing network restrictions and complex routing, finding reliable access to international platforms is difficult. Our mission is to provide transparency through technical data, helping users navigate infrastructure hurdles safely and privately.

The Audit Process

Unlike traditional review sites, we focus on the "how." We audit SSL certificates, header security, redirect chains, and mirror availability across various Australian mobile and home networks to ensure our data reflects real-world conditions.

What We Track

Infrastructure

We record server headers, WAF configurations (Cloudflare/Akamai), and encryption protocols used by each platform.

Mirror Resilience

We monitor how quickly operators deploy new mirrors when primary domains are limited by local ISP filtering.

Offer Snapshots

We capture point-in-time snapshots of bonuses and terms to provide a historical record of platform behavior.

Transparency Disclosure

SecurityAllround is a reader-supported resource. We maintain independence by conducting our own technical tests. To keep our audits free for the community, we may receive commissions from the security tools and platforms we recommend.
