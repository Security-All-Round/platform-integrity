Infrastructure & Connectivity

Mirror Domains & ACMA Blocks

Many online casinos use "mirrors" (alternative URLs) to maintain access when primary domains are blocked by the ACMA. These URLs often look like brand-99.com or play-brand.xyz. We document these in our audits to help you find a working path.

VPN Usage

If a site doesn't load on your AU home network, a VPN can often bypass local ISP filtering. However, always check the casino's terms; some platforms restrict play from certain VPN locations.

⚠️ Security Alert: Phishing & Fake Sites

Because casinos frequently rotate domains, "credential harvesting" sites often pop up. These are fake sites designed to look like the real casino to steal your login and password.

How to spot a fake site:

  • 01. Check the URL: Look for extra hyphens, misspellings, or weird TLDs (like .top, .win, .fun) that don't match known mirrors.
  • 02. Broken Links: Fake sites often only have a working Login and Register button. Check if 'Privacy Policy' or 'About' links work.
  • 03. Live Chat: Real support should be able to verify account details. Scam sites often use generic bots that dodge specific account questions.
  • 04. SSL Certificate: While fake sites have HTTPS, check the 'Issued to' info if available.

Common problems

  • Redirect blocked: Some ad blockers or DNS filters can block intermediate redirect pages. Try disabling the blocker for that page.
  • Cloudflare / 403 challenge: Some pages behave differently on CLI tools vs a normal browser. Test in a standard mobile browser.
  • ISP restriction: Availability can differ by ISP and region. Switching networks (Wi‑Fi vs mobile data) can confirm.
