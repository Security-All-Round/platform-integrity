NordPass Technical Audit

Analysing zero-knowledge architecture and credential resilience in multi-platform environments.

01. Cryptographic Specifications

Our audit focused on the implementation of the XChaCha20 cipher. Unlike standard AES-256, which requires hardware-level acceleration (AES-NI) to be performant, XChaCha20 is a stream cipher that performs exceptionally well on mobile ARM architectures.

  • [CIPHER] XChaCha20-Poly1305
  • [KDF] Argon2id (Memory-hard)
  • [KEY_LENGTH] 256-bit
  • [NONCE] 192-bit (Random)

Security Advantages:

  • • Immunity to cache-timing attacks common in software-based AES.
  • • Built-in Poly1305 MAC for authenticated encryption (AEAD).
  • • Superior performance on non-dedicated hardware.

02. Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZKP)

NordPass implements a client-side-only encryption model. During our traffic analysis, we verified that the Master Password is never transmitted in plaintext or hashed form to Nord servers. Instead, the vault is decrypted locally using keys derived via Argon2id.

Technical Verification: Captured TLS handshakes show only encrypted blobs being synced; no sensitive metadata is leaked during the synchronization cycle.

03. MFA & Biometric Resilience

We tested the integration of WebAuthn and hardware security keys (YubiKey). The protocol handles physical second-factor authentication without introducing latency into the login flow.

Technical Verdict

NordPass is our recommended standard for players managing multiple high-value accounts across international platforms.
